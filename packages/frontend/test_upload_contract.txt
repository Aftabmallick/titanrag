# TitanRAG Enterprise Security & Defense Agreement

## 1. Zero-Trust Access Control
All vector payload lookups require explicit tenant_id scoping and workspace access lists. Cross-tenant leakage is strictly prevented at the query execution boundary.

## 2. Ingestion Pipeline & Docling Parsing
Every ingested PDF, DOCX, and Markdown asset undergoes structural AST decomposition, PII entity redaction, and hierarchical chunking.

## 3. Post-Generation Verification
Generated synthetic outputs are cross-checked via natural language inference (NLI) claim verification against cited source chunks before returning to the user.
